FAQ

Questions, answered.

How realistic is the tattoo try-on?+

TattooAI maps the design onto the curves of your body and blends it with your skin tone and lighting, giving you a lifelike preview of how the tattoo will look once healed.

Can I bring AI-generated designs to my tattoo artist?+

Absolutely. Export your design in high resolution and share it with your artist as a reference. Most artists will adapt it to your anatomy and their own technique.

How accurate are the tattoo meanings?+

Meanings are drawn from widely recognised historical and cultural interpretations. Symbolism can vary between cultures and individuals, so treat them as a guide and add your own story.

Is TattooAI free?+

You can download TattooAI and explore it for free. Premium features such as unlimited generations and HD exports are available through an optional in-app subscription.

What happens to the photos I upload?+

Photos are processed only to deliver the features you use, such as try-on previews or meaning scans. You can delete your content at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.
